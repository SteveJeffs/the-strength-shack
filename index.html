What is the Shack?

Outdoor. All weather. All year.

The Strength Shack is an outdoor gym on Woodcote Road - a container, a rig, a stack of tyres and everything you need to actually get stronger.

Whether you've trained for years or never picked up a barbell, every session is coached and scaled to you. Turn up, put the work in, go home stronger.

Beginners, juniors, sports squads, people getting back into it — all ages and all abilities, same welcome.
